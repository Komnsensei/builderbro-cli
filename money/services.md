# Service menu — productized, fixed-price

Productized services: fixed scope, fixed price, fast delivery. Prospects buy
*"the thing is done"*, not *"hours of dev time."*

---

## Offer 1 — Telegram Bot, done-for-you — **$349**

**Outcome:** a customer-facing Telegram bot that answers the top N questions and
hands real requests to a human — 24/7, no dev work on their side.

**Fixed scope (7 days):**
- Bot that handles the client's 10 most-asked questions (FAQ intake + canned answers)
- Escalation to human / forwarding to a group
- Order/booking intake with structured capture
- Deploy + `@BotFather` setup, hosting included for month 1
- 14 days of free support

**Add-ons:**
- Payment collection (Stripe/crypto) — +$199
- Broadcast / announcement scheduling — +$149
- Multi-language — +$99

---

## Offer 2 — Automation Rescue — **$499**

**Outcome:** one manual workflow the client hates, fully scripted, running on a
schedule. They stop copy-pasting.

**Fixed scope (7–10 days):**
- Pick ONE workflow (scraping, form-filling, report generation, file sorting, lead gathering)
- Scripted + scheduled + logged
- Runs on their machine or a server you deploy
- 14 days of free support

**Examples that sell:**
- "Every morning someone copies data from site A into our spreadsheet" → automated
- "We monitor competitor prices but only weekly because it's manual" → daily bot
- "Lead lists come in, someone manually enriches them" → scripted enrichment

---

## Offer 3 — Agent Prototype in a Week — **$1,450**

**Outcome:** a working AI agent with real tool access (browse, search, fetch,
automate) — not a ChatGPT wrapper — solving ONE business workflow.

**Fixed scope (7 days):**
- 2 discovery calls, one workflow chosen
- Agent with 2–4 tools, MCP-compatible where useful
- Demo + handover doc + 30 days of fixes

**Who buys this:** founders and ops leads who want a proof-of-concept to show
their boss/investors before building in-house. High-ticket, low volume.

---

## Positioning rules

- Lead with the **outcome**, never the tech. "Your customers get answers at 2am" beats "I build Telegram bots with Node."
- Keep offers at 3 max. Choice paralyzes.
- When a prospect asks for something custom → quote it, but don't let it replace the menu; that's how scope creep kills productized pricing.
