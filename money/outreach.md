# Outreach kit — get clients in days

## The pattern

- **50 targeted DMs/day, 5 days/week** = 250 contacts/week. At a 5–8% reply rate
  that's 12–20 conversations; at 25–30% close rate that's 3–6 sales/week at
  offer 1–2 pricing.
- **Rules:**
  1. Personalize the first line. Reference something real (their store, their post, their bot).
  2. One question max. Never ask "are you interested?" — ask *specific* and answerable.
  3. Follow up once after 48h. Then move on.
  4. Never pitch features. Pitch the outcome (see scripts below).

## Where the prospects are (highest intent first)

| Channel | Who | Why they buy |
|---|---|---|
| Telegram groups (bot dev / e-comm / dropshipping / crypto communities) | Store owners, channel operators | They already live on Telegram; bots are native to them |
| X / Twitter — search "my Telegram" or "checking manually" | Ops people, indie founders | They complain about manual work publicly = warm lead |
| e-comm marketplaces (Shopee/Lazada/FB Marketplace sellers) | Small sellers | Manual order handling, competitor price checks |
| LinkedIn — "operations manager", "founder" at small agencies | Agency owners | They sell time; automation buys it back |
| Upwork + Fiverr | Budget buyers | Volume market; use to seed reviews, then raise prices |

## Scripts

### Script A — Telegram store owner (Offer 1)
> Hey [name] — saw your shop in [group/link]. Quick question: how do customers
> reach you right now when they want to order or ask something? If it involves
> answering the same stuff over and over, I build Telegram bots that handle it
> automatically and forward the real requests to you. $349, done in 7 days,
> hosted for the first month. Worth a look?

### Script B — manual work complaint (Offer 2)
> [name], you posted about [specific manual thing — e.g. "copying orders into a
> spreadsheet"]. I automate exactly that kind of workflow — scripted, scheduled,
> running on autopilot. Fixed $499, shipped in ~10 days. Want me to take a quick
> look and tell you if yours is automatable? No charge for the look.

### Script C — founder / ops lead (Offer 3)
> Hey [name]. You mentioned [AI thing / agent buzzword] on [platform]. Most
> "AI agents" are chat wrappers — mine browses, searches and acts through real
> tools. I build a working prototype for one of your workflows in 7 days,
> $1,450, and hand over everything. Want a 15-min call to see if it fits one
> of your processes?

### Follow-up (48h after no reply)
> Bumping this — happy to hop on a 5-min call if it's easier than typing. If
> timing's bad, no stress; I'll check back in a couple weeks.

## Outreach hygiene (do not skip)

- **Track everything** in a simple table: name, channel, script, date, replied?, $.
  Name it `outreach.csv` in this folder. Update it daily.
- **Time-box:** 90 minutes/day, morning. Same time every day. No exceptions.
- **Volume beats polish.** A "good enough" script sent 50× beats a perfect script sent 5×.
- **Iterate on the script weekly** — keep the version that gets replies, kill the version that doesn't.

## First client fast-start (if you want cash THIS week)

1. Post "I build Telegram bots, $349, 7 days, limited to 3 slots" in 3 relevant
   Telegram communities (read rules first — some ban self-promo).
2. DM 20 e-comm sellers daily using Script A.
3. Quote honestly, deliver fast, ask for a screenshotable testimonial.
4. Raise price to $499 after client #3. Repeat.
